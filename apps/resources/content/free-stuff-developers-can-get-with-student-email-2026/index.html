Category 01 · Start Here — Verification

One Application Unlocks Almost Everything Else

1
Apply first, before anything else
GitHub Student Developer Pack
The single most important application on this list. Verify with a school email, student ID, or enrollment proof and get access to 100+ partner offers in one dashboard — domains, cloud credits, IDEs, and courses. Approval typically takes a few hours to a few days.
Apply first 100+ partner offers
education.github.com/pack
2
No .edu email? No problem
SheerID Student Verification
Many perks on this list (Cursor, Notion, Canva, Perplexity) verify eligibility through SheerID rather than requiring a school-issued email — it checks enrollment databases directly or accepts a student ID upload. Worth knowing since it's usually instant.
Works without .edu email Usually instant
sheerid.com/audience-students
3
Known wrinkle right now
GitHub Copilot Student — Currently Paused
GitHub temporarily paused new sign-ups for Copilot Student, Pro, and Pro+ starting April 20, 2026 — existing subscriptions still work, but fresh activations aren't going through yet. If you're mid-verification and Copilot won't activate, that's why; the free Copilot Free tier remains available in the meantime.
Sign-ups paused Copilot Free still works
docs.github.com — Copilot for students
4
The single biggest win right now
Cursor Pro — Free for 1 Year
With Copilot Student paused, Cursor is currently the standout free AI coding tool for students — a full 12 months of Cursor Pro (normally $20/month) via SheerID verification at cursor.com/students. Use the same email to sign up and verify to avoid the most common activation mismatch.
Top pick this cycle ~$240 value/year
cursor.com/students
5
Match your email to the account
Verify With the Same Email You'll Use to Log In
The most common failure across every program on this list is verifying with your school email while logged into a different account. Create or log into each service with your school email first, then run verification — fixing a mismatch after the fact is often impossible.
Avoid this mistake Same email, every time
Log in with school email before verifying Each program checks eligibility monthly
🎯
Do the GitHub Student Pack application today, even if you don't need anything on it yet. Approval can take a few days, and several other perks on this list assume you already have it. Getting the paperwork moving early means nothing blocks you later.

No credit card required
Microsoft Azure for Students
Verified students get Azure credit that renews annually while enrolled, with no credit card needed to sign up — genuinely rare among cloud providers. Comes bundled with free tiers of popular services like App Service and Cognitive Services on top of the credit.
No card required Renews while enrolled
azure.microsoft.com/free/students
13
AWS's education program
AWS Educate
Free access to AWS training content plus credits toward compute and storage services for students, structured as a learning-first program rather than a plain credit grant. Good for anyone wanting AWS-specific experience before an internship or job that expects it.
Training + credits No card required
aws.amazon.com/education/awseducate

Verification Method — Quick Reference
Know Which System You're Dealing With
🟥 GitHub Education
School email, ID, or enrollment doc
Hours to a few days for approval
Unlocks the whole Pack dashboard
🟧 SheerID
Checks enrollment databases directly
Usually instant, sometimes needs a doc
Used by Cursor, Canva, Notion, Perplexity
🟩 Direct .edu Email
Just sign up with your school email
No document upload needed
Common for smaller partner tools
Before You Start Claiming Everything
Verification Checklist
Apply to the GitHub Student Developer Pack first — it gates several others
Create each account with your school email before running verification
Save a copy of your student ID or enrollment letter for SheerID fallbacks
Check your university's own IT page for Microsoft 365 or software deals
Set a reminder to recheck offers next semester before they lapse
